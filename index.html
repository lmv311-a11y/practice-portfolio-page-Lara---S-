Little about me

Welcome to my baking journey! As a lifelong amateur baker, my passion started in childhood, watching my mother bake delicious homemade treats. Almost every Saturday morning, I was woken up by the sweet scent of chocolate drifting through the entire house. Eventually, I began assisting her, making everything from apple tarts to chocolate cakes. She taught me everything she knew. From there, I grew a desire to explore further and challenge myself with more sophisticated desserts. For me, baking is therapeutic and has taught me many life lessons. It has trained me to look at the details, practice patience, and never give up at the first failure. Today, I am thrilled to share my love of baking with you. I invite you to join me on this wonderful adventure—please explore the options below to get started!
